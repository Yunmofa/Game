So you got past the obfuscation. Congrats, now look at what you actually have.

AN Universal is split into multiple files on purpose. Each one is connected to the others, and the script only works when all of them run together as one. Pull out a single file, deobfuscate it, and you get a piece that does nothing on its own. Missing the rest, it breaks.

Deobfuscating everything and stitching it back together takes real work, and you still would not have the right to post it. The license in this repo says you can't deobfuscate it, leak it, or repost it. If a deobfuscated copy shows up online, I will go after it: takedowns on every host, and legal action if it comes to that.

If you want to use the script, use it the normal way. If you want to show it in a video, credit "Script founded by GUI" in the description, or the video comes down.
